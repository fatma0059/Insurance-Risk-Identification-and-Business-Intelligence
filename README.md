# Insurance Risk Analysis & Business Intelligence

## Project Overview

This project focuses on insurance risk identification and analysis through a Business Intelligence solution.

The main objective is to transform raw insurance data into meaningful insights by identifying different types of risks, preparing and integrating data, and developing interactive dashboards to support decision-making in the insurance sector.

The project combines data analytics, ETL processes, data warehousing, and business intelligence to provide a structured view of insurance activities and associated risks.

## Objectives

- Identify and analyze different types of insurance risks.
- Clean, transform, and prepare insurance datasets.
- Centralize and organize data through a Data Warehouse.
- Integrate data using ETL processes.
- Develop interactive dashboards to monitor and analyze insurance risks.
- Provide a web interface to access the project's functionalities.

## Risk Categories

The project focuses on the identification of five main risk categories:

- **Behavioral Risk:** Analysis of customer behavior.
- **Financial Risk:** Identification of financial risks.
- **Fraud Risk:** Identification of potential fraud-related risks.
- **ESG Risk:** Analysis of environmental, social, and governance-related risks.
- **Global Risk:** Overall risk assessment.

## Project Workflow

1. **Data Identification:** Identification of relevant insurance data and risk indicators.
2. **Data Cleaning & Preparation:** Data cleaning and transformation using Python.
3. **ETL & Data Integration:** Extraction, transformation, and loading of data using Talend.
4. **Data Warehousing:** Organization and centralization of insurance data.
5. **Business Intelligence:** Development of interactive Power BI dashboards.
6. **Web Application:** Development of a web interface to access the solution.
